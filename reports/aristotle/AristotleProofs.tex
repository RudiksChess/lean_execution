\begin{abstract}
This document collects, for reference, the complete Lean~4 proofs that the automated theorem-proving
agent \emph{Harmonic Aristotle} produced when asked to reconstruct the main results \emph{cold} ---
given only the definitions and statements, with the human proofs withheld. They are reproduced here
verbatim (only the report's anchor comments are removed). Each was verified under the same
Lean~v4.29.0 toolchain as the rest of the project, compiles with no \texttt{sorry}, and depends only on
the standard classical axioms \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and \texttt{Quot.sound}.
Discussion of how these compare to the hand proofs is in the two main reports
(\emph{Completeness of Propositional Natural Deduction} and \emph{Verifying the Correctness of
Quicksort}); this document is just the raw, machine-checked evidence.
\end{abstract}
